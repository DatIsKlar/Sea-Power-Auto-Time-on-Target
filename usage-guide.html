Firing a Coordinated Strike How to make your missiles arrive on target together — from a whole formation, or from a single ship carrying different missiles.

Open the planner

A button sits top-center: TOT PLANNER. Click it or press Alt+G to open the panel. The window is draggable and shows everything below.

Target

Whatever enemy contact you last selected. The panel remembers it.

Shooters

Your last-selected friendly ship, or its whole formation via the toggle.

Each missile row shows a checkbox, the missile type & count, its ETA (flight time to the target) and range, and a salvo stepper. The FIRE — TIME ON TARGET button staggers every checked missile so they land together; Fire now launches them uncoordinated.

A formation (several ships)

A group of ships, one enemy, everything splashes at once.

  1. Select the target

    Click the enemy ship you want to hit.

  2. Select one of your ships

    Click any ship in the formation you want to fire.

  3. Open the planner & tick "whole formation"

    Alt+G. Turn on the whole formation toggle so every missile ship in that formation is listed.

  4. Check the missiles & FIRE

    Tick the missiles you want (all on by default), then press FIRE — TIME ON TARGET. Slower/farther missiles launch first; faster/closer ones later.

One ship, different missiles

A single ship carrying two missile types of different speeds — fire both so they hit together.

In the planner

Slow missile — higher ETA

e.g. a subsonic sea-skimmer. Longer flight time — the mod fires it first.

Fast missile — lower ETA

e.g. a supersonic ASM. Shorter flight time — the mod holds it and fires it later.

  1. Select the target

    Click the enemy.

  2. Select your ship

    Leave the whole formation toggle OFF (this ship only).

  3. Open the planner

    Alt+G. You'll see both missile types listed with different ETAs.

  4. Check both & FIRE

    Tick both missiles, press FIRE — TIME ON TARGET. They converge.

If they seem to launch together

Check the ETA column. If both missiles show the same ETA (same missile type, or similar range) there's nothing to stagger — that's correct. Also, under high time compression a real stagger of, say, 15 sim-seconds passes in a blink, so it looks simultaneous but the impacts are still synced. Slow time down to watch it.

Confirm it's working

Turn on the play-by-play to see the math.

Set VerboseLogging = true in
BepInEx/config/com.seapowermods.autotot.cfg, restart, then watch BepInEx/LogOutput.log during a strike:

[AutoTOT] coordinating 2 missile launch(es) on <target>: longest enroute 210.0s, impacts synced.
[AutoTOT] launched <ship> -> <target>      (slow one, now)
   ...later...
[AutoTOT] launched <ship> -> <target>      (fast one, delayed)

Good to know

Grouping is by target. Missiles aimed at different enemies aren't coordinated with each other — each target gets its own synchronized group.

Normal fire is unaffected. The planner only fires when you press its buttons. Attacking normally (outside the panel) works as usual — unless you switch on Auto-coordinate normal group orders (the checkbox in the panel, or Alt+T), which is OFF by default.

It syncs across orders, not within one salvo. A single order of, say, 4 identical missiles still leaves the launcher at its normal salvo interval — same-speed missiles fired seconds apart arrive seconds apart. Coordination is between different orders / missile types.

Estimate, not a guarantee. Flight time is range ÷ missile max speed in a straight line (ignoring boost/cruise phases and target movement), so arrivals are tightly clustered rather than a perfect single instant on very long shots.
